## What this tests

C3-small tests one change to C2's covariance: each satellite's is rescaled in each of three directions from how its past forecasts
compared with later published precise orbits, and widened or narrowed each day by how well that day's fit matched its data.

On GNSS the fits themselves were changed first, by C3-A11, on two registered results: GLONASS on a published area seed that includes the arrays, and Galileo FOC with a fixed Y acceleration. C2's class covariance is applied over those fits.

It is judged on 44 non-maneuvering high-orbit satellites (5 laser spheres, 14 GLONASS, 25 Galileo), pooled, on days C2 never scored,
without operator-notice spans or storm days (1 forecast in 5). The 6 lower spheres are only reported (too few, errors move together).
History and the daily chi-square reference use only files published before the cutoff. The fit track is the latest arc ending by the cutoff, as if available at once. Earth orientation is measured, not predicted, in every window, so predicted Earth-orientation error is not in this test; its size per lead is reported. Truth comes from arcs starting at or after the fit's end, excluding the fit's own arc; for GNSS that is the next daily file, which may share tracking data with the fit's. A pass does not mean real time.

Weather: R2-M takes observed weather and is labeled; R2-O takes what was issued by E; R3 has no drag and its weather rows are inert (P3 checks this). Answer 2026-12-21, or 2027-01-20 if the freeze check needs December.

## What a pass means

"Realistic outside storms, pooled over 44 named satellites, from precise orbits." It does not mean each satellite (one three times off
passes 89 %), radar or GP data, maneuvering objects or real time.

If R3 stops at 11-30, no plane reaches |beta| >= 75 deg in the scored windows. A pass then says nothing about that regime, where calibration shows -0.7 to -1.1 m normal error on GLONASS at 6 h.

It does not show that later fits can stand in for those orbits, because calibration found they cannot here: the variance of forecast minus later fit is 0.018 to 0.18 of forecast minus orbit on GLONASS's normal axis at 1 to 12 h, and 1.9 to 3.5 of it on the radial axis at 1 h on every family (`precheck-P-REF-family.json`).

On GLONASS's normal axis the scale is learned from the same ESA files that score it, and on calibration it reads 14-25 % low in variance against GFZ at 1-48 h.

Once each satellite is scaled, errors are narrower than Gaussian (per-window variance of d2/3 0.42 to 0.53, against 0.67), so one scale cannot center both the mean and 95 % coverage, and the band favors coverage. A pass means the 95 % ellipsoid holds about 95 %, with mean d2/3 in band, and on these errors that mean sits above 1: 1.12 to 1.18 where coverage is exactly 0.95, and 1.09 to 1.30 as the model stands on the gate's samples (1.08 to 1.24 at the fresh mix). So by the mean the covariance runs tight in sigma, by about 6-9 % at 95 % coverage and 4-14 % as it stands. P8 failed and stays failed on the record: at the measured scales R3's median e^û/e^u is 0.62 to 0.97, so û itself runs low, most of all along-track.

On the measured error shape a correct covariance, meaning its 95 % ellipsoid holds 95 %, passes 95 %. One 40 % too large, 30 % too small or 20 % too small passes 0 %, and one 25 % too large 3 %. If fresh windows look like calibration and validation, the model as frozen passes 76 %. An unannounced maneuver can sink a correct one: at the upper bound of the rate P2 measured it passes 6 % with the registered shape and 90 % with the measured event's shape (section 4).

## 2. Model (nothing else)

`P_w(L) = D_o g_w(L) P_C2(L) D_o + C_w`: P_C2 is C2's frozen class covariance (R3; R2-O, R2-M); C_w is C2-A1's NSGF term, R2 only (P4 checks its axes). C3-A9's kappa is withdrawn (C3-A10).

- **GNSS fits, re-based (C3-A11):** GLONASS, all 14: the published seed 0.0325046 m2/kg (A1: Duan et al. 2020, J. Geod. 94:72, Table 5: arrays 30.85 + largest body surface 4.53 = 35.38 m2 / 1415 kg x 1.3), prior sigma 100 % of the seed as before, C2's receipt left alone. Galileo FOC, 22 of the 25: fixed FORCES.ECOM2.Y0_M_S2 = +6.85e-10 m/s2 in FFM's axes (eY = unit(eD x r)), in the request from an identity table checked against the SINEX block, through the Y-bias experiment's adapter (`lib/y0.mjs`, pinned in `build.json`). Galileo IOV (38857, 37846, 37847), the spheres and R2 are unchanged. GLONASS's -0.086 nm/s2 is not applied.
- **(a)** `g_w(L) = (chi2nu_w / chi2nu~_o(E))^(a0 + a1 log10 L)`, unclamped; chi2nu~_o(E) is the median chi2nu of o's fits available at E within 90 d (at least 5, else the class median).
- **(b)** `D_o = diag(e^(u_k/2))` (RTN, per lead), `û_k = argmin 1/2 sum_j [e_jk^2 / (e^u a_jk + c_jk) + ln(e^u a_jk + c_jk)] + (n0/4) u^2`, with a_jk = (g_j P_C2,j)_kk and c_jk = C_j (0 on R3). New code `axes-mle-ref` (gp-error-model's axes-mle lacks c and the prior), in the E2E. No switch by class, axis, lead or family (C3-A8).
- **History** j: cutoffs in (E - 90 d, E - 3 d]. e_j = forecast minus truth at E_j + L, the same arc-disjoint truth the score uses. Usable if each of the fit's and the reference's files meets max(last epoch + lambda, Last-Modified) <= E: lambda NSGF 3 d, ilrsa 11 d (Last-Modified 10.5 d after span end, 260912-261003), ESA0OPSFIN 14 d after its GPS-week batch. - **The reference arm, report only (C3-A8):** C3-b-ref and C3-ab-ref, with their own constants and k_ref, on cal/val and at scoring, beside the verdict, with no rule attached. There ẽ_j = forecast minus a later FFM fit to the same product over the 24 h centered on E_j + L, shifted to the first allowed epoch (mid-arc from 12 h; no state of the fit's own arc), and c_jk = (P_ref,j + C_j)_kk, with P_ref,j = k_ref x the reference fit's a posteriori covariance at E_j + L; k_ref per class, axis and lead group (1-12 h, 24-72 h) by maximum likelihood of reference minus truth (P-REF); k_ref = 1 is reported. Reference fits keep running in history and fresh windows: that fresh figure is direct evidence for objects that have only a later fit.

Earlier windows still serve as history and are reported, not fitted (C3-A9). a0 -0.2 to 1.2 (step 0.1), a1 -0.8 to 0.8 (0.2), refined; n0 in {0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50}.
