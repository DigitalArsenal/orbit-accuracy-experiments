# Scoreboard: round 0 against the final construction (stack issue 312)

Exploratory. Generated by the development runner's scoreboard from its per-set rows (kept on the machine); aggregates only. Module sdn-ap 0.6.5
(`f0b8352a43f3`). 72 h error = RMS of 3D position over the 72 h after the epoch, median over sets, km. Ratios: the plan's paired
statistic (median of log ratios, 95 % interval from resampling satellites, `lib.mjs`). "Closer" = share of sets where the served record
beats the baseline. Day 1..7: the served record's ratio to the set by day.

Round 0 = the registered construction (served: D5, else B3). Final = `final-construction.json`: an orbit-product input (CPF prediction, GNSS ultra-rapid or rapid orbit) when one was out
by t0 (P13 CPF, U2 ESA ultra-rapid, R3 GFZ rapid), else the class's public-set construction (MEO K12, GEO KG8c, HEO H5F7, HIGH_LEO
KH8, MID_LEO KM5, LOW_LEO D5), else B3. Period B has no orbit-product input (CPF prediction, GNSS ultra-rapid or rapid orbit) except the CPF files of 3 low satellites, so B is nearly a public-set result.
