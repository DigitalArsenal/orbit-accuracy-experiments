# G1: SDN-GP against FFM-propagated Starlink and precise orbits (baselines)

Generated by `experiments/sdn-gp-starlink-ffm-match/tables.mjs` from recorded window rows. Position errors are 3D RMS in km unless a column says otherwise; every cell is a median over windows. Fit interval 24 h; held-out day k is (24 h + (k-1) d, 24 h + k d].

## Method in brief

- **Truth.** Starlink: the satellite's SpaceX forecast is fitted over 0-48 h (never across the 48 h join) by `analysis/od fit_ffm` (FFM-1, NRLMSISE-00 drag with s = 0 because no $DCL is published yet, kappa x drag folded into B_eff, Cr*A/m locked), then FFM-1 propagates the fitted state for 96 h at 60 s. That trajectory is the truth of the `FFM-propagated truth` row; the `SpaceX forecast` row fits the same two theories to SpaceX's own states (24 h) and scores day 1 inside its span, strictly before the 48 h join. Precise orbits: ESA final GNSS, GFZ MGEX rapid GEO and IGSO satellites (2025), GFZ rapid GNSS orbits of 2026-10-11 to 10-22, ILRS combined weekly and NSGF arcs (the archive's and, for September and October 2026, those fetched from NASA CDDIS), NASA ISS OEM, windows as in the sgp4-xp measurement.
- **Windows.** 24 h fit from the first state; SGP4 by `fit_elements` (six mean elements and B*), SGP4-XP by `propagator/sgp4-xp fit` (those and BTERM, AGOM). One window per Starlink; the other sources use the strides of `config.json`.
- **SDN-GP.** Side B's module `sdn-gp-propagator` (theory 16), used as built and by its published surfaces only: `fit_sdngp` fits the six elements, a signed effective ballistic term B_eff, AGOM and the two density time-variation terms to the same 24 h of truth (the truth as a plain $OEM naming its frame; the run's saved space-weather table; no density correction and no prior), and the batch entry `sdngp_propagate_minutes_soa` propagates the fitted set to the held-out epochs (TEME), which `foundation/frames` takes to the truth's axes before the same `pair_statistics` scoring as XP. The module is rebuilt in place by its author, so every row belongs to a build named by the SHA-256 of its wasm (section 2a); the cells above are build 7ea0ea272bbf. Scoring check: over the 1128 windows with three comparable held-out days the pair_statistics RMS of the days agrees with the module's own held-out RMS (its COMMENT) to a ratio of 0.829 to 1.126 (median 0.998). SpaceX's own forecast could be fitted only for the same-day capture: the server's file had changed since the registered fit for 199 of the registered seats, and a changed file is not the file the truth was fitted to.
- **RIC.** SGP4: `element_residuals` (RTN of the truth). SGP4-XP: the module's own fit split for the fit, and the fitted set propagated by the module and compared by `pair_statistics` for the held-out days (at most one state per 5 minutes). Components are the median over windows of each window's component RMS, so they do not sum exactly to the 3D median.
- **RIC check.** Over all held-out days of all windows the three components recombine to the module's own 3D RMS to 0.8643 to 1.2257 for SGP4-XP (the thinning to one state per 5 minutes) and 1.0000 to 1.0000 for SGP4.
- **Environment.** Earth orientation: the local IERS daily finals of 2026-10-13. Space weather: the `data-source/space-weather` rows (GFZ, NOAA SWPC) through 2026-10-25, then NOAA SWPC 45-day daily Ap and F10.7 for the eight days after (Kp and the 81-day average held), because the module's rows end where its Kp forecast ends and the truth runs 96 h. Planetary kernel DE440s. Density correction s = 0 (no $DCL is published yet). Hashes are in `manifest.json`.
- **COMSPOC XP (local scoring only).** Their published SGP4-XP sets, propagated through the black-box module and compared with the same-day truth; their set epochs are 1 to 5 days older than the truth, so their errors are at those ages, not at the 24 h of the fitted arms. Never fitted to, never an input; aggregates only.
- **Plan.** `experiments/sdn-gp-starlink-ffm-match/PLAN.md`, frozen by its first commit, with amendments A1 (headline table), A2 (start states, weather extension, the COMSPOC scoring arm), A3 (fit length), A4 (GEO and IGSO windows from the GFZ MGEX set found after registration), A5 (spacecraft truth from NAIF: highly elliptical orbits scored, cislunar, lunar and heliocentric awaiting SDN-GP) A6 (truth quality: the iteration cap and the zero bound) A7 to A7d (the LEO fit floor against zonal-only gravity, DESIGN.md M1 to M3: section 12) A8 to A8c (current GNSS truth from the GFZ rapid orbits, TESS from MAST, CAPSTONE, Artemis I and Danuri from JPL Horizons, windows prepared for SDN-GP: section 1 and section 13) and A9 to A9e (Psyche, Lucy and Juno from Horizons, current laser-ranging truth from the ILRS products on CDDIS, the SDN-GP arm and its rules, its fit-length arm, the lowering variants and the fit span against COMSPOC).
- **Not measured here.** SDN-GP on the cislunar and heliocentric truth (the lunar windows are scored in section 14; the other packs and windows are prepared, section 13); SDN-GP's covariance (section 5); the NAIF kernels of Psyche, Lucy and Juno are SPK type 1, unreadable by `files/orbit-products` 0.1.4, so that truth comes from JPL Horizons vector tables; GEO operator ephemerides (SES, Intelsat) are not stored; the theory-term ranking (phase 3).

## 11. Notes for the modules (found here, not changed here)

- `analysis/od fit_ffm` (T1a): (a) B_eff >= 0 leaves 52 of 239 fits at the zero bound (orbit raising and other net thrust cannot be represented; their truth is a drag-free trajectory); (b) 56 of 239 fits stop at the 12-iteration default; refitted with a cap of 30 on 16 of them (8 of 9 converge and 5 of 7 converge, about 16.0 iterations), the truth moves by a median of at most 0.000 km at 96 h and, for the 11 fits that reproduce SpaceX to 2 km, by at most 0.000 km, so the stopping rule or the default cap is too strict; (c) 19 objects need B_eff above 0.5 m2/kg, more than ten times a physical value (continuous lowering or decay, no discrete event detected): they fit SpaceX to a median of 2 to 11 km and are 2 km (median) from a newer file's first hours; a single B_eff cannot represent them.
- `data-source/space-weather` rows end where the Kp forecast ends (about two days ahead) and `propagator/ffm` refuses any arc past them (`space-weather-out-of-range`); this experiment appended NOAA SWPC 45-day Ap and F10.7 itself (amendment A2).
- `files/orbit-products` 0.1.4 reads SPK types 8, 9 and 13 only: the JPL navigation kernels of Psyche, Lucy and Juno are type 1 (`unsupported-variant`), the Juno merge also holds type 2 (`unsupported-spk-materialization`), TDRSS is type 10 (generated from two-line elements). Its epochs are TDB, so every caller needs `foundation/time` (done here, one call per epoch).
- `sdn-gp-propagator` (side B), found by using it: (a) the invoke surface's `propagate` stops one day after the set's epoch, so the held-out days were scored through the batch entry `sdngp_propagate_minutes_soa` after `ingest_omm`; (b) `fit_sdngp` and `ingest_omm` read plain (not size-prefixed) buffers, and take the frame of an $OEM from the string `REFERENCE_FRAME.NAME`: a block that names GCRF only through the enumeration is read as TEME, silently, and the fit then reports a good RMS in the wrong frame (30 to 40 km errors against the truth); (c) the build is rebuilt in place from a working tree, so a result is only reproducible with the copy of the wasm it ran (section 2a).
- `fit_sdngp` rule 2c.2 (a B_eff above 0.5 m2/kg is replaced by tau0, held after the arc) on the 19 Starlinks with such a B_eff (section 2b): the rule gives day 1 / day 3 median 5.1 / 170 km, free B_eff day 1 / day 3 median 0.6 / 6.5 km, SGP4-XP day 1 / day 3 median 13.3 / 25.4 km, SGP4 day 1 / day 3 median 10.3 / 45.2 km; the build as shipped gave day 1 / day 3 median 0.6 / 6.5 km (rule applied to 0 of 19).
- GNSS MEO (GFZ rapid), held-out day 3 median: SDN-GP 0.128 km, SGP4-XP 0.118 km (fit RMS 0.013 against 0.021 km): SDN-GP is behind XP in extrapolation at this altitude although it fits tighter.
- `fit_sdngp` fits that did not converge or were refused, by regime: GEO, BeiDou-2 and QZSS-3 (GFZ MGEX rapid): 6 of 90; IGSO, BeiDou and QZSS (GFZ rapid, 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-22): 1 of 30; HEO, Artemis II high-Earth-orbit checkout (one short arc: 8 h fit, 12.85 h held out as day 1): 1 of 1.

## 11. The LEO fit floor against zonal-only gravity (DESIGN.md M1 to M3)

Plan amendments A7 to A7d. Each window is one start state: 132 clean Starlink truths (converged, 0 < B_eff <= 0.5 m2/kg) at their registered start states, 12 ISS windows (the registered NASA OEM, state turned EME2000 to GCRF), 12 LAGEOS-1 and LAGEOS-2 windows (first state of the weekly ILRS orbits of 2026-07-19 to 2026-08-23, drag off). Every variant is fitted by SGP4 and SGP4-XP over 24 h, 21.7 h and 34.2 h and scored on the three held-out days as in section 1; the difference series between variants are taken by `pair_statistics` and summarized by a quadratic plus one least-squares sinusoid. ### 11g. What it means for the fit

- **The floor is gravity.** In-track fit RMS of best-case XP with the full field against the zonal field only: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 401 against 8 m; ISS (51.64 deg) 406 against 9 m; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 119 against 2 m. - **Day 3 is gravity.** The held-out day-3 error of the same fits, full field against zonal only: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) XP 8.08 against 0.61 km, SGP4 11.32 against 0.63 km; ISS (51.64 deg) XP 11.65 against 0.56 km, SGP4 15.75 against 0.58 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) XP 0.36 against 0.03 km, SGP4 1.10 against 0.06 km. The zonal-only values are the ceiling for a theory that carries the m-daily terms and fits the same 24 h.
- **J22 alone.** Degree-2 tesserals only (`tess2`), XP day 3 at 24 h: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 6.25 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 7.83 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 0.43 km.
- **A longer fit, no new physics.** XP day 3 at 24 h against 34.2 h (full field): Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 8.08 against 4.78 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 11.65 against 5.90 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 0.36 against 0.27 km; SGP4: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 11.32 against 5.39 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 15.75 against 6.18 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 1.10 against 0.96 km. At 21.7 h XP has no drag term, so the same change is large only where the truth has no drag (`full-nodrag`) and is a loss where it has.
