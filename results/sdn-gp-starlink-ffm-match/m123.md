## 11. The LEO fit floor against zonal-only gravity (DESIGN.md M1 to M3)

Plan amendments A7 to A7d. Each window is one start state: 132 clean Starlink truths (converged, 0 < B_eff <= 0.5 m2/kg) at their registered start states, 12 ISS windows (the registered NASA OEM, state turned EME2000 to GCRF), 12 LAGEOS-1 and LAGEOS-2 windows (first state of the weekly ILRS orbits of 2026-07-19 to 2026-08-23, drag off). FFM-1 propagates it for 120 h at 60 s under EGM2008 70x70 (`full`) and under FFM's own truncations: `zonal` (order 0, the zonals to degree 70), `tess2` and `tess6` (zonals plus the tesseral and sectorial terms to degree 2 or 6), `order14` and `order15` (orders up to 14 and 15; their difference is the 15th order alone), and, for Starlink and the ISS, `full-nodrag` and `zonal-nodrag` (drag off). Everything else in the force model (third bodies, solid and ocean tides, relativity, Earth radiation, SRP, and drag where it is on) is the same in every variant, so a difference between two variants is the gravity terms that were dropped. Every variant is fitted by SGP4 and SGP4-XP over 24 h, 21.7 h and 34.2 h and scored on the three held-out days as in section 1; the difference series between variants are taken by `pair_statistics` and summarized by a quadratic plus one least-squares sinusoid. The `full` re-propagation reproduces the registered truth to 0.0e+0 km (largest difference over 132 Starlinks).

### 11g. What it means for the fit

- **The floor is gravity.** In-track fit RMS of best-case XP with the full field against the zonal field only: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 401 against 8 m; ISS (51.64 deg) 406 against 9 m; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 119 against 2 m. The rest of the force model is the same in both, so the difference is the tesseral and sectorial terms and nothing else.
- **Day 3 is gravity.** The held-out day-3 error of the same fits, full field against zonal only: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) XP 8.08 against 0.61 km, SGP4 11.32 against 0.63 km; ISS (51.64 deg) XP 11.65 against 0.56 km, SGP4 15.75 against 0.58 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) XP 0.36 against 0.03 km, SGP4 1.10 against 0.06 km. The zonal-only values are the ceiling for a theory that carries the m-daily terms and fits the same 24 h.
- **J22 alone.** Degree-2 tesserals only (`tess2`), XP day 3 at 24 h: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 6.25 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 7.83 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 0.43 km.
- **A longer fit, no new physics.** XP day 3 at 24 h against 34.2 h (full field): Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 8.08 against 4.78 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 11.65 against 5.90 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 0.36 against 0.27 km; SGP4: Starlink 48 to 60 deg (53 deg shell) 11.32 against 5.39 km; ISS (51.64 deg) 15.75 against 6.18 km; LAGEOS-1 (109.84 deg) 1.10 against 0.96 km. At 21.7 h XP has no drag term, so the same change is large only where the truth has no drag (`full-nodrag`) and is a loss where it has.
