Inputs and their licences: [../../data/e13/SOURCES.md](../../data/e13/SOURCES.md).

**Planet-derived rows stay on the machine.** Planet's states are CC BY-NC 4.0, so no state, no per-satellite-day fit and no per-arc row derived from them is committed: the per-arc rows of the arcs, of the
rescaling and of the Cr·A/m sensitivity are kept local only, in the derived store outside the repository (`/opt/data/sdn-archive/e13-open-dca`), and the files here hold aggregates (statistics, intervals, counts). The one
exception is in the history: `05f069d` committed 20 per-arc rows in `dev/cr-sensitivity.json`; `6b0ec4d` took them out of the tree, and the history is not rewritten (the owner's data-licence policy of 2026-10-10:
public copies with any licence question come down, local copies are kept). SpaceX's files are never written to disk at all; the collector stores derived fits and pair mismatches only.
