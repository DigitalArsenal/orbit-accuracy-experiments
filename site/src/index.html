      Verification · HPOP Against Orekit 13.1

      Locked Down Force By Force

      63 cases on identical states, forces and constants: five orbits, ten force sets, and the IERS 2010, VCM and atmosphere additions. Each point is the largest position difference over a day of hourly samples. The reference is Orekit 13.1 with its de Sitter term evaluated in consistent frames. Pick a case and re-run it here.

      VCM Parity · analysis/vcm-adapter

      A Vector Covariance Message, Round Trip

      An SP Vector Covariance Message read into a PRW request, propagated with its covariance, and written back as a VCM. The adapter transforms the equinoctial covariance exactly; the sigmas it recomputes match the ones the message prints. The format leaves the units of the ballistic coefficient’s row unstated, so both readings run.
